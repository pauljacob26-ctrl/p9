About Solar Sphere

Welcome to Solar Sphere

Solar Sphere is an educational space dedicated to exploring the Sun and the planets that orbit around it. Discover the unique features of each planet, their moons, and other fascinating objects that make up our solar system.

Discover the Wonders of Space

Space helps us understand our place in the universe and inspires us to ask big questions. Explore the planets, learn about the Sun, and discover the science behind our cosmic neighbourhood.
